Space Saver

Find large videos and convert them to H.265 using your Intel GPU. Audio, subtitles, attachments and chapters are copied; originals are retained until you delete their backups.

Encoding settings & GPU diagnostics

If a source codec cannot be decoded by your GPU, disable hardware decoding to use CPU decoding with GPU encoding. No automatic software fallback is performed.

Video re-encoding is lossy. Start with one representative file. HDR, Dolby Vision, interlaced and multi-angle videos are currently rejected to protect their presentation.
